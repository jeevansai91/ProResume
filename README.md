# ProResume
A Modern Resume Builder for Students
ProResume is a modern, user-friendly resume builder designed to help students and jobseekers create standout, ATS-friendly resumes in just a few minutes. With a curated set of beautiful templates tailored for top Indian colleges and a lightning-fast, intuitive interface, ProResume makes building a professional resume smarter, faster, and easier than ever before.

✨ What Does ProResume Do?
Guided Resume Creation: Choose from 12 expertly designed templates representing different professions and top colleges (like IIT Kanpur, IIT Bombay, IIM, NIT, etc.).
Build From Scratch or Import: Upload your previous resume (PDF/DOC/DOCX) or build a new one from scratch using a simple step-by-step wizard.
Instant Preview & Download: Live preview as you fill, and download your resume as a high-quality PDF.
Modern, Mobile-Friendly UI: Works perfectly on both desktop and mobile – no sign up required to start!
Easy Navigation: Clear home navigation, with the ability to return to the start anytime.

🎓 Why Is It Useful for Students?
Saves Time: No more wrestling with Word docs or design tools—create a polished resume in minutes.
Designed for YOUR Journey: Templates match real student career paths, including engineers, designers, analysts, researchers, and more.
ATS-Friendly: All resumes are formatted to survive automated company screenings.
Pre-Written Examples: Get a jump start with sample skills, roles, and university details relevant to Indian and global career paths.
No Coding or Subscription Needed: Instant access, open source, and 100% free to use.
